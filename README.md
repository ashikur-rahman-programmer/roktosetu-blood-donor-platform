# 🩸 RoktoSetu – Blood Donor Network Bangladesh

> **"রক্তের প্রয়োজনে বন্ধু যেমন পাশে থাকে, সেতু হয়ে বাঁচায় জীবন।"**

**RoktoSetu** is a community-driven blood donation network designed to bridge the gap between voluntary blood donors and recipients across Bangladesh. During medical emergencies, finding the right blood group nearby can be stressful and time-consuming. RoktoSetu simplifies this process by providing a fast, reliable, and localized search platform.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to save lives by creating a seamless connection between blood donors and patients in critical need. We aim to make blood donor discovery instant, accessible, and transparent for every district and upazila in Bangladesh.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Smart Donor Search:** Search donors instantly filtering by **District (জেলা)**, **Upazila (উপজেলা)**, and **Blood Group**.
- **Real-Time Availability:** Donors can toggle their availability status (_Ready to Donate_ vs _On Cooldown_) to ensure accurate search results.
- **Emergency Blood Request:** Post urgent requirements with patient details, hospital location, needed units, and target timeline.
- **Eligibility Tracker:** Automated countdown timer showing when a donor becomes eligible for their next donation (90 days post-donation).
- **Direct & Secure Contact:** Direct calling or messaging system to connect with donors quickly in critical situations.

## 🩸 Blood Group Compatibility Guide

| Blood Type | Can Give To                  | Can Receive From |
| :--------- | :--------------------------- | :--------------- |
| **A+**     | A+, AB+                      | A+, A-, O+, O-   |
| **A-**     | A+, A-, AB+, AB-             | A-, O-           |
| **B+**     | B+, AB+                      | B+, B-, O+, O-   |
| **B-**     | B+, B-, AB+, AB-             | B-, O-           |
| **AB+**    | AB+ _(Universal Recipient)_  | Everyone         |
| **AB-**    | AB+, AB-                     | A-, B-, AB-, O-  |
| **O+**     | A+, B+, AB+, O+              | O+, O-           |
| **O-**     | Everyone _(Universal Donor)_ | O-               |

---

## 📋 Basic Guidelines for Donors

- **Age:** 18 – 60 years old
- **Weight:** Minimum 45 – 50 kg
- **Interval:** Minimum 3 to 4 months gap between donations
- **Health Requirements:** Must be in good physical health, well-hydrated, and rested before donation.
